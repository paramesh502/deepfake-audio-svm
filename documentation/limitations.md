# Limitations and Known Issues

## Model Limitations

### 1. Generalisation to Unseen Deepfake Generators
The SVM is trained on the acoustic artefacts of specific TTS/VC systems present
in the ASVspoof 2019 LA dataset (attacks A01-A19). A deepfake generated by a novel
neural vocoder (e.g., VITS, NaturalSpeech 2, VALL-E) that was not present during
training may evade detection if its artefacts lie outside the learned decision boundary.

### 2. Domain Mismatch
Performance may degrade for audio recorded under conditions different from training:
- Different microphones or recording environments
- Heavy background noise not seen during training
- Aggressive MP3/codec compression
- Different languages (model trained primarily on English speech)

### 3. Short Recordings
Recordings shorter than 0.5 seconds are rejected. Very short recordings (0.5-1s)
provide insufficient context for reliable feature estimation, especially for pitch
and temporal features.

### 4. Non-Speech Audio
The model may produce unreliable predictions on:
- Music (classified as speech-like but articulatory features do not apply)
- Ambient noise or silence
- Whispered speech (very low energy)
These cases trigger the speech quality warning.

### 5. Probability Calibration Limitations
Platt scaling assumes the relationship between decision scores and probabilities
is well-modelled by a sigmoid. This may not hold for all feature distributions.
The Expected Calibration Error (ECE) measures but cannot eliminate this.

### 6. SVM Scalability
The from-scratch linear SVM uses mini-batch subgradient descent which may require
many epochs to converge on large datasets. The sklearn SVC(RBF) with n > 50,000
samples becomes very slow due to quadratic kernel matrix computation.

## Dataset Limitations

### 7. Speaker Overlap Risk
The ASVspoof 2019 protocol was designed to avoid speaker overlap between train/dev/eval,
but the target speakers of voice conversion attacks may overlap. This is documented in
the protocol files and noted in our experimental setup.

### 8. Synthetic Generator Overlap
Training and evaluation may share spoofing systems. We use the official protocol split
and do not evaluate on training data, but acknowledge that test generators partially
overlap with training generators in the ASVspoof 2019 setting.

## Deployment Limitations

### 9. Colab Runtime Dependency
The Gradio website is hosted on the Colab runtime. If the runtime disconnects (after
~90 minutes of inactivity in free tier), the website becomes unavailable. For permanent
hosting, the model must be deployed on a persistent server.

### 10. Not a Forensic Tool
This system should NOT be used as sole evidence in any legal, security, or disciplinary
proceeding. It is a research prototype built for academic purposes. Human expert review
is required for any consequential decision.

## Ethical Considerations
- False positives (genuine speech classified as fake) can harm innocent speakers.
- False negatives (deepfakes classified as genuine) allow harmful content to pass undetected.
- The system does not provide speaker identification.
- Probabilities should be communicated with appropriate uncertainty framing.
